# MLAI Brand Voice

How MLAI sounds. The voice is the product promise made audible: precise, private, and unwilling to over-claim.

---

## The voice in one line

**An engineer who respects you enough to tell you the truth, including the parts that are still a target.**

---

## Five principles

### 1. Claim only what you can defend
Every number is tagged by how it's known. In prose this shows up as careful verbs: a measured result *is*; a target is something we're *building toward*; a reported figure is *cited*. Never let a goal wear the clothes of an achievement.

> ✅ "The 295× figure is tagged a target — a benchmark-track objective, not a measured result."
> ❌ "WDBX delivers 295× faster matrix math."

### 2. Plain over impressive
Explain the mechanism, then name it — not the other way around. The reader should understand *why* before they're handed the acronym.

> ✅ "A query enters at the top, greedily walks toward its nearest neighbor, drops a layer, and repeats. The result is logarithmic search. This structure is HNSW."
> ❌ "WDBX leverages state-of-the-art HNSW for blazing-fast ANN."

### 3. Short sentences carry weight
The voice favors declarative sentences with a strong subject and verb. Long technical clauses are broken up. Rhythm does the emphasis that bold text would otherwise have to.

> ✅ "Most AI runs in someone else's cloud. MLAI is built on the opposite premise."

### 4. Privacy is a property, not a promise
Never say "we take your privacy seriously." Say where the computation physically runs. The voice grounds trust in architecture, not assurances.

> ✅ "Privacy stops being a policy you have to trust and becomes a property of where the computation physically runs."

### 5. No hype words, no Apple implications
Banned: "revolutionary," "game-changing," "blazing-fast," "synergy," "best-in-class." Also banned: anything implying Apple partnership or endorsement. The approved Apple framing is the only Apple framing.

## Tone by surface

| Surface | Tone |
|---|---|
| **Product pages** | Technical, confident, mechanism-first. Assume a smart reader who can handle detail. |
| **Homepage** | The thesis, distilled. Slightly more declarative and quotable; still no hype. |
| **Investors** | Sober and specific. Targets labeled as targets. The bet stated as a bet. |
| **Company** | Warmer, narrative. The origin-as-parable is allowed here. |
| **Contact / Services** | Direct and human. "Mail goes to engineers," not "submit your inquiry." |

---

## Do / Don't quick reference

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Tag every metric's provenance | Round a target up into a result |
| "Built on Apple's public frameworks" | Imply Apple partnership or endorsement |
| Explain the mechanism, then name it | Lead with the acronym |
| Use short, declarative sentences | Stack three clauses and a buzzword |
| Say where computation runs | "We take privacy seriously" |
| Apache-2.0, Zig 0.17-dev | "open source" (vague), stale versions |
| Attribute quotes to no real person | Put words in a named individual's mouth |
